Majestic clouds of the cosmos

Nebulae are one of the most stunning cosmic phenomena, acting as vast clouds of gas and dust where new stars ignite and old ones fade.

Some of the beautiful nebulae

Helix Nebula
NGC 7293
A planetary nebula formed by an expanding shell of gas from a dying star.

Omega Nebula
M17
An emission nebula acting as a star-forming region glowing from ionized gas.

Horsehead Nebula
Barnard 33
A dark nebula consisting of a dense dust cloud blocking background light.

Crab Nebula
M1
A supernova remnant made of an expanding debris field powered by a central pulsar.
